# Dot & Key — Brand Launch Intelligence

## 📊 Project Overview

An end-to-end marketing and customer analytics project built to evaluate the performance of a Dot & Key brand launch.

The project analyzes sales, customers, products, marketing channels, campaigns, advertising spend, conversions, revenue, and profitability to identify business opportunities and areas for improvement.

## 🎯 Business Objectives

- Identify the highest-performing marketing channels
- Measure marketing efficiency using ROAS
- Analyze campaign conversion performance
- Identify profitable and loss-making products
- Understand customer segment and customer type performance
- Analyze revenue across cities
- Identify factors influencing profitability
- Translate analytical findings into actionable marketing recommendations

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

- **Excel** — Data source and initial exploration
- **SQL (SQLite)** — Business and marketing analysis
- **Python** — Data analysis and exploration
- **Pandas** — Data manipulation and aggregation
- **Power BI** — Interactive dashboard and visualization
- **DAX** — Marketing KPI calculations
